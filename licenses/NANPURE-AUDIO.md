## Project-created recordings

Erase B, Scanner D (Sonar arc), Reveal E (Crystal reveal) and Arrow F (Prism pass) are original local synthesized NANPURE WRLD effects. No third-party recordings were sampled in their synthesis.

## Processing and exact asset identity

The owner-approved effects are copied byte-for-byte from the audition files. Their original/prepared hashes, lead trims, gain, excerpt offsets/durations and default 72% playback volume are retained in `assets/audio/approved/config.json`. Reusing one effect for several actions does not copy or reinterpret it. Tile selection remains silent.

Background music uses the complete owner-supplied AndriiH recording, with no rearrangement or replacement composition. It is encoded as AAC 128 kbps stereo/44.1 kHz with MP4 fast-start and encoder-delay metadata, reducing 4,387,735 bytes to 2,230,198 bytes. Original and encoded SHA-256 hashes are in the same manifest. Default playback volume is 16%, independently adjustable. Native media looping retains the original musical opening and ending; no invented notes or crossfaded overlapping players are added.
